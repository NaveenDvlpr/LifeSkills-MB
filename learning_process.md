##### Learn how to learn
1. Everyone is unique. Understanding isn't enough for mastering a skill. Understanding along with practise on various circumstances results mastery.
2. Active mode of the brain gets activated when we focus on something like reading a newspaper, solving a coding problem. Passive mode of the brain gets activated we are relaxed like going to a casual walk, sitting idle.

##### Learn any skill in x hours.
1. Destructure the skill or task.
2. Understand what it requires and have the tools to do it.
3. Learn to auto correct and follow the goal.
4. Be comfortable being uncomfortable.
5. Do it until you own it.
