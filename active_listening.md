### Answers

##### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?
1. Pay attention to the speaker
2. Let the speaker complete their thoughts and respond
3. Paraphrase and check with the speaker for better understanding
4. Take Notes
5. Show that your listening with body language
6. Use door openers

##### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?
Reflective is a subset of active listening. It is a communication strategy where a listener improves their understanding of a speakers idea by providing the information back to confirm the idea has been understood correctly.
* Mirroring body language and emotions
* Verifying the message.
Empathetic approach towards a speakers idea.

##### What are the obstacles in your listending process?
* Gets distracted in my thoughts
* Interrupting when someone is speaking
* Not listening to the speaker completely before responding


##### What can you do to improve your listening?
* Paying attention to the speaker.
* Responding when the speaker completes.
* Taking notes of important conversations
* Paraphrasing and preseting the understanding of the idea to the speaker.

##### When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?
* Offering my belongings without fully mentioning how I intend them to use it.
* When someone asks help when there is no time.
* Accepting tasks instead saying no in non-shift time.

###### When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

* Rarely when stress crosses the bearable limit.
* Being rude with our beloveds.

##### When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?
